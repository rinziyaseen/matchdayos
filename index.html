Phase 03

The MatchdayOS Planning Framework

Programmatic planning is not linear. Standard calculators assume pricing scales evenly. Real systems do not. This framework models two opposing market forces to deliver realistic expectations.

1 Force 1: Scale Efficiency

Larger investments unlock bulk purchasing power across DSP channels, reducing base cost-per-thousand (eCPM) levels as delivery models find ambient pricing avenues.

Scale_Discount = max(0.75, min(1.0, BudgetVolume ^ -0.05))
Real World Example: Scale Discount
At $10,000 Spend: 0% Discount (Base eCPM)
At $200,000 Spend: 21% CPM Savings (0.79x Multiplier)

2 Force 2: Saturation & Attention Decay

Forcing higher frequency targets over long windows leads to banner fatigue and bid-rate inflation because the DSP has to bid aggressively on restricted, same-user inventory auctions.

Fatigue_Premium = 1.0 + (0.08 * max(0, Freq - 1.0)) * Duration_Penalty
Real World Example: Fatigue Premium
At 1.5x Freq Cap: Minor +4% Auction Premium
At 10x Freq Cap: Extreme +72% Auction Markup
